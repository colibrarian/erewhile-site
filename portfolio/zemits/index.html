The task

Help salons compare devices worth thousands of euros

Salon owners and cosmetologists do not buy a laser or an RF platform on impulse. They compare technologies, prices and what support they get after the purchase.

The site had to present the whole device range in three languages, show real treatment results and make it easy to ask a question or book training.

What we built

From first look to a request in one flow

Device catalogueEach device has a card and a detail popup with price, features and photos, on the home page and a full catalogue page.
Training and seminarsMasterclasses and seminars are part of the offer, so buyers see they will not be left alone with the device.
Results and reviewsBefore-and-after photos and client reviews build trust for a technical, high-ticket product.
Search-ready in three languagesLanguage versions with hreflang, structured data for every device with its price, a sitemap and link previews for each page.
Visual language

Clinical blue with an editorial touch

A deep blue palette signals medical precision, and an elegant serif for headlines keeps it from feeling like a hospital. A clean sans handles prices, specs and buttons.
