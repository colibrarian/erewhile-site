The task

Sell experience, not just a CV

Restaurant owners hire a consultant when something is going wrong. They need to trust the person quickly and understand what he will actually do.

The site had to tell a 30-year career story in a few screens and turn it into clear services and a way to get in touch.

What we built

Story, services, approach, contact

Career storyA short path from line cook to consultant, with the formats and projects behind it.
ServicesRestaurant launches, kitchen systems and consulting explained in plain terms.
ApproachHow he works with a team and an owner, so expectations are clear before the first call.
Contact in three languagesA language switch and a contact form plus WhatsApp for quick questions.
Visual language

Dark kitchen with a gold edge

A near-black base with warm gold accents feels like a serious kitchen after service. A wide display face gives the name weight, and a neutral sans keeps the story readable.
