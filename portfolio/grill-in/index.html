The task

Three businesses, one simple page

A restaurant, a takeaway and a water park attract different visitors with different questions: what is on the menu, when can I pick up, how do I book.

The site had to answer all three without becoming confusing on a phone.

What we built

Clear sections for each reason to visit

Grill barMenu highlights and the lakeside atmosphere.
TakeawayOpen the menu, choose dishes and set a pickup time.
Water parkWhat the park offers and a booking call to action.
LocationMap, free parking and directions to Mežaparks.
Visual language

Coal, ember and gold

Dark coal backgrounds with ember orange and gold accents bring the feel of an open grill. A tall condensed display face for headings, a friendly sans for menus.
