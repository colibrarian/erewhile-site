The task

Make a last-minute bouquet order easy from a phone

Flowers are often bought in a hurry: for a birthday tonight or a meeting tomorrow. A long checkout loses those customers.

The shop needed a site where people see ready bouquets, understand delivery, and send an order in a few taps, in Latvian or Russian.

What we built

See it, pick it, send it

Bouquet galleryReady bouquets with photos, so customers choose by eye, not by description.
Order through WhatsAppThe chosen bouquet goes into a WhatsApp message, where the shop confirms details and the address.
Delivery zonesFree delivery areas and prices across Riga are clear before ordering.
Local search basicsFAQ, map, opening hours, structured data and Latvian and Russian versions for local search.
Visual language

Bright, fresh and a little playful

Soft violet and pink accents on white match the mood of the bouquets. A characterful serif for headings and a neutral sans for prices and practical details.
