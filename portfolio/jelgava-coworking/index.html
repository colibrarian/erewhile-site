The task

Show the space and the prices before people visit

People choosing a coworking want two answers fast: what the place feels like and how much it costs.

The site had to show the atmosphere, explain who the space is for, lay out the packages clearly and collect applications in three languages.

What we built

Atmosphere first, then a clear choice

Atmosphere galleryLarge photos of the space and the meeting room set the mood in the first seconds.
Packages with pricesFocus, Flow, Studio and Business plans, a day pass and meeting room rates side by side.
Who it is forFreelancers, IT, photographers, students and entrepreneurs each see themselves in the offer.
Application form and FAQEvery package leads to one form, and common questions are answered on the page.
Visual language

Calm white with one strong red

White space and black text keep the site as quiet as the space itself. One deep red from the logo marks actions and prices. A single modern grotesque runs through everything.
