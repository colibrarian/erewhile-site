The task

One site for the organisation and all its projects

An NGO speaks to several audiences at once: young people who want to join, partner organisations, and funders who need to see results.

Each Erasmus+ project also needs its own space for programme, travel, participants and outputs that stays online after the project ends.

What we built

Organisation on top, projects underneath

Events and callsUpcoming programmes and an "apply" path right from the first screen.
Vision and aboutWhat UNTU stands for, in clear language for young people and partners.
Project sectionsFTS and DWCM have their own pages: programme, travel info, participants, library, media and learning modules.
Apply and partner formsOne form for participants and one for organisations that want to collaborate.

A dark background with a bright yellow accent gives the NGO energy without looking corporate. Bold Montserrat headings and a dragon character make it recognisable.
