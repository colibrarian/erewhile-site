Common questions
Good to know before you start
How fast can the site go live?
Solo Launch and Dual Reach are typically delivered within 7–14 business days after we receive your content and brief.
What do I need to provide?
Your logo (or we'll discuss direction), photos or existing visuals, and a description of what you do. If you're unsure how to phrase things, we'll help you structure the copy.
What if my domain costs more than €15?
You only pay the difference. If your preferred domain is €22, your contribution is €7. We handle the purchase and transfer everything to you.
Can I add more pages later?
Yes. Additional pages are €120 each for both Solo Launch and Dual Reach. Extra language versions for Dual Reach are €50 each.
What about hosting?
We set up reliable, fast hosting as part of the build. Any ongoing hosting costs are transparently agreed upfront — typically very low for a one-page site.
Is this right for an event or pop-up?
Absolutely. Solo Launch was built with events in mind — fast turnaround, clear info, form for RSVPs. Mention your deadline in the form and we'll prioritise.
