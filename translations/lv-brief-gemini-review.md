## Systemic Changes Across the Text

* **«Vajag ...» → «Vajadzīga / vajadzīgs / vajadzīgi ...» and debitive (`jā-`)** — replaced colloquial *vajag* with more natural written UI phrasing (LV-BR-048, LV-BR-050, LV-BR-083, LV-BR-086, LV-BR-100, LV-BR-101, LV-BR-131, LV-BR-135).
* **«Bankas saite» → «Internetbanka»** — replaced the literal calque of *Bank link* with the standard term used by Latvian businesses (LV-BR-071).
* **«neesat pārliecināts» → «neesat pārliecināti»** — adjusted to the gender-neutral polite *jūs* form (LV-BR-010).
